Each resample draws n observations from the sample with replacement — some points are picked more than once (they stack), others not at all. The mean of each resample drops into tier 3; the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of that pile give the 95% interval. Raise the skewness and watch the bootstrap interval lean while the normal-theory interval stays stubbornly symmetric.
